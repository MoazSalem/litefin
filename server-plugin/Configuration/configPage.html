Litefin Client Hosting

Open Litefin Client

The Litefin Client is hosted directly from your Jellyfin server at /litefin. It features hardware-accelerated playback with the Movi WebAssembly video player and ultra-fast navigation.

Choose how the quick-access Litefin launcher appears on the Jellyfin Web top bar beside your profile avatar. A browser cache clear or hard refresh (Ctrl+F5) might be needed after changing this setting.
Supplies Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy and Cross-Origin-Embedder-Policy headers required for WebAssembly multi-threading (SharedArrayBuffer) and hardware video demuxing. Enabled by default.
